The internet has become scarier than ever, data-tracking, phishing, cyberbullying. Everything on the web is not so private anymore.

If you’re going to post make sure you do one of these few things to keep yourself safe:

  1. Don’t post where you live (even the city)
  2. Make sure your posts are not offensive in any way
  3. If you are 17 or younger keep your account private
